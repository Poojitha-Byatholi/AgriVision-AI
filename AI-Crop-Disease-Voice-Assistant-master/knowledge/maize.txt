Maize is a kharif crop grown during rainy season.
Maize can also be grown in rabi season under irrigation.
Maize grows well in warm climate.
Maize requires well drained fertile soil.
Maize requires moderate irrigation.
Maize does not tolerate waterlogging.
Nitrogen is important for maize growth.
Apply nitrogen fertilizer at knee high stage of maize.
Leaf blight is a common disease of maize.
Leaf blight causes drying of maize leaves.
Downy mildew affects maize plants.
Seed treatment helps control maize diseases.
Balanced fertilizer improves maize yield.
Timely weeding is important for maize crop.
