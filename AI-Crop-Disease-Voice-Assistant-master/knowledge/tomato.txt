Tomato is a vegetable crop grown in multiple seasons.
Tomato can be grown in kharif and rabi seasons.
Tomato grows well in warm climate.
Tomato requires well drained loamy soil.
Tomato requires regular irrigation.
Overwatering can cause root rot in tomato.
Early blight is a common disease of tomato.
Early blight causes brown spots on tomato leaves.
Late blight affects tomato leaves and fruits.
Use fungicides to control tomato blight.
Nitrogen fertilizer supports tomato growth.
Apply potassium fertilizer for better fruit quality.
Timely staking improves tomato yield.
