Potato is a rabi crop grown in cool climate.
Potato is grown from October to February.
Potato grows well in sandy loam soil.
Potato requires well drained soil.
Potato requires moderate irrigation.
Avoid waterlogging in potato fields.
Late blight is a major disease of potato.
Late blight causes leaf and tuber damage.
Early blight affects potato leaves.
Seed treatment helps prevent potato diseases.
Apply nitrogen fertilizer during early growth.
Apply potassium fertilizer for tuber development.
Proper spacing improves potato yield.
