Wheat is a rabi crop grown during winter season.
Wheat is mainly grown from October to March.
Wheat grows well in cool climate.
Wheat requires well drained loamy soil.
Wheat requires moderate irrigation.
Four to six irrigations are required for wheat.
Nitrogen fertilizer is essential for wheat growth.
Apply nitrogen fertilizer at tillering stage of wheat.
Apply phosphorus fertilizer during sowing of wheat.
Rust is a common disease of wheat.
Rust disease causes yellow or brown spots on wheat leaves.
Smut disease affects wheat grains.
Smut disease spreads through infected seeds.
Seed treatment helps prevent wheat diseases.
Excess moisture can increase fungal diseases in wheat.
Timely irrigation improves wheat yield.
