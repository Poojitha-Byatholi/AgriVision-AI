Rice is a kharif crop grown during monsoon season.
Rice is mainly grown from June to November.
Rice requires warm and humid climate.
Rice grows well in clayey and loamy soil.
Rice requires standing water during early growth stages.
Rice requires frequent irrigation.
Nitrogen fertilizer increases rice yield.
Apply nitrogen fertilizer in split doses for rice.
Blast disease is a common disease of rice.
Blast disease causes leaf and neck damage in rice.
Brown spot disease affects rice leaves.
Use resistant rice varieties to prevent diseases.
Proper drainage reduces disease spread in rice.
Excess nitrogen may increase pest attack in rice.
Timely transplanting improves rice productivity.
